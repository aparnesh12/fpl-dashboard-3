How this works

Wildcard/Free Hit: independently optimized 15-man squads (integer linear programming, not a heuristic guess) against your real budget and real constraints. Wildcard maximizes the next 5 gameweeks since those picks persist; Free Hit maximizes just the next one since it reverts after a week. Bench Boost/Triple Captain use your CURRENT squad instead — those chips don't let you rebuild, so the question is which upcoming gameweek suits the players you already have.

How this works

Predicted Risers/Fallers use FPL's own official Price Change Predictor (new for 2026/27): progress toward tonight's threshold, which can run past 100% — FPL's own guidance is 100%+ is "very likely," 80%+ is "likely." It's still a guide, not a guarantee — a burst of late transfer activity can flip the outcome right up to the deadline. Today's/Season Risers/Fallers are changes that have already happened, straight from FPL's price-tracking fields.
