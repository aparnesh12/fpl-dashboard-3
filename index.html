How this works

Ranked by predicted points over the next 5 gameweeks — form × fixture difficulty × minutes reliability, plus a history adjustment for players who've faced their next opponent before (pulled from the last two completed seasons' real results, not a guess). That adjustment only kicks in with at least 2 prior meetings, and is capped so it nudges the ranking rather than dominates it. Newly promoted opponents (Coventry, Ipswich, Hull) won't have a history entry yet — there's no top-flight data for them to draw on. Players already in your squad are marked.

Squad Rating

Add your Team ID to config.json to rate your squad.

How this works

Two independently optimized 15-man squads, built from scratch against the full player pool using your current squad value + bank as the budget — same constraints as a real wildcard or free hit (2 GKP / 5 DEF / 5 MID / 3 FWD, max 3 players per club). Wildcard maximizes predicted points over the next 5 gameweeks, since those picks stick around. Free Hit maximizes just the next gameweek, since it reverts after one week — a genuinely different squad, not the same one relabelled. This is a real optimizer (integer linear programming), not a heuristic guess.
